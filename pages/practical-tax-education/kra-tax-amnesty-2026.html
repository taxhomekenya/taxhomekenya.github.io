WHAT IS THE TAX AMNESTY?

The Tax Amnesty Programme gives qualifying taxpayers an opportunity to regularise their tax affairs and seek relief from associated penalties, interest and fines.

Where a taxpayer has outstanding principal taxes relating to periods up to 31 December 2025, the taxpayer may still qualify for relief on the related penalties and interest, subject to the requirements of the programme.

Important deadline: The 2026 Tax Amnesty Programme runs until 31 December 2026. Taxpayers should not wait until the final days to review their records or make arrangements.

WHO MAY QUALIFY?

If you have outstanding principal taxes relating to periods ending on or before 31 December 2025, you may qualify for relief on the associated penalties and interest, provided that the applicable programme conditions are met.

Eligibility should be assessed using the taxpayer's actual iTax position, tax records and the specific liabilities outstanding. The existence of a liability does not by itself guarantee that every penalty, interest amount or tax period will qualify.

WHAT SHOULD YOU DO?

  1. Log in to your iTax account.
  2. Review your outstanding tax liabilities.
  3. Establish which liabilities qualify for amnesty.
  4. Reconcile the principal tax, penalties and interest against your returns, payment receipts and accounting records.
  5. Make arrangements to settle the qualifying principal tax before the deadline and retain evidence of payment or an approved arrangement.

WHY SHOULD YOU ACT EARLY?

  • You have time to correct inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • You can separate principal tax from penalties and interest before making payment arrangements.
  • You reduce the risk of last-minute iTax, payment or documentation problems.
  • You can obtain professional assistance where the liability history is complex.
  • You improve your overall tax compliance position.
