ACCRUED EXPENSES: WHAT HAPPENS WHEN THE BILL ARRIVES?

Practical Tax Education | 5 November 2026
Tax Home Kenya Limited

An accrued expense is an expense that relates to the current financial year but has not yet been invoiced or paid by the reporting date.

For example, electricity consumed in December when the bill only arrives in January.

LET'S LOOK AT AN EXAMPLE

A business estimates that electricity consumed by 31 December 2026 amounts to KSh 45,000. However, the bill has not arrived.

1. At 31 December 2026 — Recognise the expense

The electricity relates to 2026, so it must be recorded in that financial year.

AccountDebit (KSh)Credit (KSh)
Electricity Expense45,000–
Accrued Expenses–45,000

What does this achieve?

  • The 2026 expenses increase by KSh 45,000.
  • A liability of KSh 45,000 is recognised in the statement of financial position.

2. In January 2027 — The actual bill arrives

Assume the electricity bill arrives for KSh 45,000.

Record:

AccountDebit (KSh)Credit (KSh)
Accrued Expenses45,000–
Trade Payables–45,000

This clears the accrual and recognises the supplier liability. The January invoice does not create a second electricity expense because the expense was already recognised in December.

3. What if the actual bill is KSh 48,000?

The difference of KSh 3,000 is an additional expense to be accounted for in January, subject to the appropriate accounting treatment.

Important: Do not record the full January bill as a new expense without considering the accrual already recognised. Otherwise, you may double-count the electricity expense.

OTHER COMMON ACCRUED EXPENSES

  • Unpaid salaries.
  • Audit fees.
  • Interest incurred but not yet paid.
  • Water and telephone bills.
  • Professional fees for services already received.

PRACTICAL TAKEAWAY

At year-end, identify expenses already incurred but not yet invoiced. Make reasonable estimates supported by records, contracts, meter readings or other available information.

Accurate accruals help ensure expenses are recognised in the correct financial year.
