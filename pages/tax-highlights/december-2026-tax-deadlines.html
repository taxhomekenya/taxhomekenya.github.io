CORPORATION TAX — INSTALMENT TAX

The following taxpayers have instalment tax payments falling due on 20th December 2026, depending on their accounting year-end:

  • 1st Instalment (25%) — businesses with accounting periods ending in August 2027.
  • 2nd Instalment (25%) — businesses with accounting periods ending in June 2027.
  • 3rd Instalment (25%) — businesses with accounting periods ending in March 2027.
  • 4th Instalment (25%) — businesses with accounting periods ending in December 2026.

Due: 20th December 2026

AGRICULTURAL INSTALMENT TAX

Businesses deriving more than two-thirds of their income from agricultural, pastoral, horticultural or similar activities follow a different instalment payment arrangement.

  • 1st Instalment (75%) — qualifying agricultural businesses with accounting periods ending in March 2027.
  • 2nd Instalment (25%) — qualifying agricultural businesses with accounting periods ending in December 2026.

Due: 20th December 2026

CORPORATION TAX — BALANCE OF TAX

Balance of Tax for businesses with accounting periods ending in August 2026.
Due: 30th December 2026

CORPORATE TAX RETURNS

Self-Assessment Income Tax Returns for businesses with accounting periods ending in June 2026.
Due: 30th December 2026

IMPORTANT NOTES

  • Late filing and payment may attract penalties and interest.
  • NIL returns must be filed where applicable.
  • Instalment tax generally applies where the expected tax payable exceeds KES 40,000, subject to applicable rules.
  • Persons subject to Turnover Tax are not required to pay instalment tax.
  • Reconcile your records before filing and making payments to avoid discrepancies and unnecessary KRA queries.
