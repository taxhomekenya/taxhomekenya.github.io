Withdrawals & Winnings: What Has Changed?

Issue No. 005 | July 31, 2026
Tax Home Kenya Limited

The Finance Act 2026 reintroduces taxation of winnings from betting, lotteries, gaming and prize competitions. This marks a significant shift in the tax landscape for the gaming industry in Kenya.

Key Changes in Taxation

  • Focus on Winnings: The focus of taxation is now on winnings, not withdrawals. This means that tax is calculated based on the amount won rather than the amount withdrawn from the betting account.
  • Point of Payment: Tax applies at the point of winnings payment. The betting or gaming company is responsible for withholding the tax at the time the winnings are credited to the player.
  • Withholding Tax (WHT): WHT will now be imposed on gross winnings payable.
Important Note: Both players and gaming operators need to be aware of these changes to ensure full compliance and avoid unexpected tax liabilities.

Stay Informed

The taxation of winnings is a complex area, and the reintroduction of these rules requires careful attention to detail. More updates and practical examples will be shared soon to help you navigate these changes.
