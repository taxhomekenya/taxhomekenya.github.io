VAT Invoices: VAT Can Only Be Charged on Taxable Supplies

Finance Act, 2026 | Section 42(2), VAT Act | Issue No. 048 | October 2, 2026
Tax Home Kenya Limited

What Has Changed?

The Finance Act, 2026 amended Section 42(2) of the VAT Act to clarify when an invoice may show an amount that purports to be tax.

An invoice showing an amount that purports to be tax shall only be issued in respect of a taxable supply.

What Does This Mean?

VAT should only be shown or charged on an invoice where the underlying supply is a taxable supply for VAT purposes.

Businesses should therefore confirm the VAT treatment of their goods and services before charging VAT on an invoice.

What Businesses Should Check

  • Whether the goods or services supplied are taxable for VAT purposes.
  • Whether the transaction is zero-rated, exempt or outside the scope of VAT.
  • Whether the invoice accurately reflects the VAT treatment of the underlying supply.
  • Whether accounting and invoicing systems apply the correct tax code.

Key Point

An invoice should not show an amount purporting to be VAT where the underlying supply is not taxable.

Source: Finance Act, 2026 — Section 42(2) of the VAT Act.
