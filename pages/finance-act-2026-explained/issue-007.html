Tax Amnesty Is Back: Clear Your Tax Arrears Before 31 December 2026

Issue No. 007 | September 15, 2026
Tax Home Kenya Limited

The Finance Act, 2026 has reintroduced a Tax Amnesty Programme to give taxpayers a fresh opportunity to regularize their tax affairs.

If you have outstanding tax liabilities relating to periods up to 31 December 2025, this is your chance to reduce the burden of penalties and interest. The amnesty runs until 31 December 2026.

Who Qualifies?

The amnesty applies to taxpayers with tax liabilities relating to periods ending on or before 31 December 2025.

What Is the Benefit?

Eligible taxpayers can have penalties and interest waived, provided they meet the conditions set out under the programme, including settling the outstanding principal tax within the prescribed timeline.

Why Should You Take Advantage?

  • Save money by eliminating accumulated penalties and interest.
  • Improve your tax compliance status.
  • Avoid future enforcement actions by KRA.
  • Start afresh with a clean tax record.

Deadline: 31 December 2026

Missing this window could mean losing the opportunity to benefit from the amnesty.
